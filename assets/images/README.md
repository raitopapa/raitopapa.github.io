# Image Asset Intake

Place approved photos in the folders below. Prefer WebP for production use.

## Folders

- `hero/` - wide hero backgrounds and large section images.
- `tools/` - tool card thumbnails.
- `use-cases/` - audience/use-case photos such as street trees, parks, home gardens, and field work.
- `blog/` - article images.

## Naming

Use lowercase ASCII filenames:

- `hero-tree-canopy.webp`
- `tool-diagnosis-leaf.webp`
- `tool-pruning-branches.webp`
- `use-civic-street-trees.webp`
- `blog-deadwood-check.webp`

## Requirements

- Avoid people, car plates, nameplates, private addresses, and identifiable homes.
- Use your own photos or photos with clear commercial-use permission.
- Keep original photos outside the repository if they are large; commit optimized WebP/JPEG only.
- Recommended width: hero 2000px, cards 900px, blog 1200px.
